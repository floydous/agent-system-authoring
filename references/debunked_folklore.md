# Debunked Prompting & Tool Schema Folklore

## Executive Summary
This catalog separates prompting and schema conventions into:
1. **Refuted Conventions (`T1`–`T5` / `Methodology`)** contradicted by retrieved literature, vendor specs, statistical methodology, or deconfounded Phase B experiments (`PB-1`–`PB-6`); and
2. **Inconclusive / Conditionally Bounded Claims** where Phase B measurements refined the operational scope.

---

## 1. Refuted Conventions

| Folklore / Industry Convention | Conventional Belief | Verified Reality | Supporting Evidence & Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Assign an expert persona ('You are a Fields Medalist...') to boost factual or math accuracy"** | Authoritative role framing improves objective problem-solving accuracy. | **Does not improve overall accuracy over a neutral no-persona prompt.** Zheng et al. found zero overall gain across 162 roles, 4 LLM families, and 2,410 questions. In non-ceiling `PB-5` (`reasoning_effort="low"`), `neutral_objective` scored `29/30` (`96.7%`, `TARa@5 = 83%`) vs `expert_persona` `27/30` (`90.0%`, `TARa@5 = 50%`, Fisher `p = 0.6120`). | **T5** (`R-SYS-06`): Basil et al. ([arXiv:2512.05858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05858)); Zheng et al. ([arXiv:2311.10054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10054)); `PB-5`. |
| **"Always write 'Think step-by-step' on reasoning models"** | Explicit CoT phrases improve all complex prompts universally. | **Unnecessary and can hinder performance on native reasoning models.** Reasoning models deliberate internally before answering; Li et al. also observed consistent instruction-following drops under CoT prompting across 15 models on IFEval/ComplexBench. | **T3** (`R-SYS-07`): OpenAI [Reasoning Best Practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices); Li et al. ([arXiv:2505.11423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11423)). |
| **"Long-context LLMs handle arbitrarily large tool catalogs without pruning"** | Large context windows eliminate selection degradation from distractor tools. | **Selection accuracy drops sharply as tool count and response size grow.** LongFuncEval measured `7%` to `85%` performance drops as tool catalogs scaled and `7%` to `91%` drops as tool responses lengthened; Anthropic explicitly advises consolidating tools. | **T4** (`R-TOOL-08`): Kate et al. ([arXiv:2505.10570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10570)); Qin et al. ([arXiv:2307.16789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16789)); Anthropic `define-tools`. |
| **"Setting `temperature=0` guarantees deterministic single-run results"** | Greedy decoding produces reproducible outputs without repeats. | **Non-determinism persists due to FP16/BF16 non-associativity and dynamic batching.** Atil et al. measured up to `15%` accuracy variation and `70%` best-worst gaps across 10 runs. Phase B repeats on `cx/gpt-6-luna` confirmed run-to-run drift: `TARa@5` fell to `17%` (`PB-1 flat_markdown`, `PB-4 affirmative_only`) and `50%` (`PB-5 expert_persona`). | **Methodology / T4** (`R-SYS-10`): Yuan et al. ([arXiv:2506.09501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09501)); Atil et al. ([arXiv:2408.04667](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04667)); `PB-1`, `PB-4`, `PB-5`. |
| **"Never state negative prohibitions—replace them with affirmative-only wording"** | Telling a model what to do instead lets you omit explicit `Do NOT` exclusions cleanly. | **Affirmative-only wording failed over half the time on high-frequency lexical exclusions.** In `PB-4` (`N = 30`/arm), `affirmative_only` dropped to `14/30` (`46.7%`, 19 violations) vs `bare_negative` `27/30` (`90.0%`, `p = 0.0006`) and `paired_affirmative` `30/30` (`100.0%`, `p = 1.944e-06`). Explicit prohibitions remain essential when excluding specific tokens/words. | **T2** (`R-SYS-05`): `PB-4` ([stats_recomputed.md](../evidence/stats_recomputed.md)). |
| **"More few-shot examples always improve compliance"** | Adding many examples monotonically boosts performance regardless of rule alignment. | **Excessive examples can degrade performance, and conflicting examples override written rules.** Tang et al. demonstrated over-prompting degradation; Camassa & Shiller measured `27%`/`43%` grand-mean rule following across 13 models when assistant turns contradicted rules; OpenAI advises trying zero-shot first on reasoning models. | **T5** (`R-SYS-09`): Tang et al. ([arXiv:2509.13196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13196)); Camassa & Shiller ([arXiv:2605.20382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20382)); OpenAI Reasoning Guide. |
| **"LLMs neutrally ignore promotional fluff when choosing tools"** | Tool selection depends purely on functional capability matching. | **Massive preference hijacking confirmed on both standard LLMs and `cx/gpt-6-luna`.** Faghih et al. showed >`10x` usage shifts across 17 LLMs. In deconfounded `PB-2`, assertive claims flipped selection of an identical tool from `0/30` (`0.0%`) to **`30/30` (`100.0%`, Fisher `p = 1.691e-17`)**, whereas adding a neutral usage example alone caused `0/30` shift (`p = 1.0`). | **T2 / T5** (`R-TOOL-04`): `PB-2` ([stats_recomputed.md](../evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Faghih et al. ([arXiv:2505.18135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18135)). |
| **"Constrained JSON decoding has zero reasoning cost when pre-emission thinking is absent"** | Parameter field order in strict schemas never affects computed arguments. | **Forcing constrained action fields before explanation tokens severely degrades accuracy without native reasoning.** When `reasoning_effort = "none"` (`reasoning_tokens = 0`) in `PB-6`, both `no_rationale` and `rationale_last` scored **`11/30` (`36.7%`)**, whereas `rationale_first` achieved **`30/30` (`100.0%`, Fisher `p = 5.343e-08`)**. | **T2 / T4** (`R-TOOL-11`): `PB-6` ([stats_recomputed.md](../evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Tam et al. ([arXiv:2408.02442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02442)); Nguyen et al. ([arXiv:2601.07525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07525)). |
| **"Typed JSON Schema properties make parameter descriptions unnecessary"** | Types (`"type": "string"`) and parameter names convey sufficient format info. | **Non-universal formatting rules fail without explicit descriptions.** In `TS-2`, universal ISO timestamps (`15/15`) and ISO currencies (`12/15`, `p = 0.2241`) were mostly inferred, but an unstated no-space `"lat,lon"` rule failed **`0/15` vs `15/15` (`p = 1.289e-08`)**. | **T2** (`R-TOOL-05`): `TS-2` ([stats_recomputed.md](../evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Lu et al. ([arXiv:2408.04682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04682)). |
| **"XML containers inherently break OpenAI-lineage reasoning compliance"** | Wrapping rules in XML tags drops accuracy by 15% compared to Markdown headers. | **Phase A `SP-4` artifact caused by a `[P0\|P1\|P2\|P3]` wording bug.** Phase A's `XML_TEXT` omitted the `"without brackets"` phrasing (`4/12` `c5_severity` vs `12/12` in `RAW_TEXT`). Once wording was equalized in Phase B (`PB-1`, `N = 30`/arm), `flat_markdown` (`0.972`), `flat_xml` (`0.983`, `p = 0.3632`), and `nested_xml` (`0.994`, `p = 0.1019`) showed no XML penalty on `cx/gpt-6-luna`. *(Note: He et al. `2411.10541` still showed Plain Text/Markdown/JSON/YAML template sensitivity up to `40%` on GPT-3.5-turbo.)* | **Refuted internal artifact** (`R-SYS-08`): `PB-1` ([stats_recomputed.md](../evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); He et al. ([arXiv:2411.10541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10541)). |

## 2. Conditionally Bounded / Statistically Inconclusive Comparisons

| Hypothesis | Observation Across Phase A & Phase B | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **`paired_affirmative` vs `bare_negative` lexical avoidance (`PB-4`)** | `paired_affirmative` hit `30/30` (`100.0%`, `TARa@5 = 100%`) vs `bare_negative` `27/30` (`90.0%`, `TARa@5 = 83%`), a `+10.0 pp` directional edge (`p = 0.2373` Fisher exact, `p = 0.3632` paired t over 6 prompts) that does not reach `alpha = 0.05`. | ⚠️ **Inconclusive (`p = 0.2373`)** |
| **Trailing reminders on high-effort reasoning models (`PB-3`)** | `cx/gpt-6-luna (xhigh)` maintained `30/30` (`100%`) compliance even under ~1,200 words of noisy log distractors. Prospective memory degradation (`Mittal, 2026`) remains **T5** for standard/lower-effort models, unchallenged by `PB-3`. | ⚠️ **Ceiling on `xhigh`** |
