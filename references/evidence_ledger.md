# Master Evidence Ledger: System Instructions, Tool Schemas & Skill Packaging

## Overview
This ledger indexes all 28 authoring rule IDs (`R-SYS-01`–`R-SYS-10`, `R-TOOL-01`–`R-TOOL-12`, `R-SKILL-01`–`R-SKILL-06`) alongside their verification tier, newest models tested in primary sources, empirical observations across **Phase A** (`228` runs, `N = 1`) and **Phase B** (`480` runs, `N = 5`) on `cx/gpt-6-luna`, and primary citations verified in [source_checks.md](../evidence/source_checks.md).

## Evidence Tier Policy
- **T1 Verified fact**: A specification constraint or API mechanic confirmed directly against source text retrieved on 2026-11-22.
- **T2 Measured on gpt-6-luna**: A statistically significant effect recomputed directly from raw execution traces in `evidence/` via `evidence/recompute_stats.py`.
- **T3 Current vendor guidance**: A recommendation quoted from official vendor documentation retrieved on 2026-11-22 (*not measured by us*).
- **T4 Literature — mechanism**: A peer-reviewed finding grounded in architectural mechanics that have not changed (left-to-right/constrained decoding, BPE tokenization, context-token cost, floating-point/batching non-determinism). Valid across model generations regardless of test date; models tested are still listed.
- **T5 Literature — learned behavior**: A peer-reviewed finding whose effect depends on model training (instruction following, personas, few-shot sensitivity, positional bias, tool-description influence). Kept as a rule and tagged with tested models (`transfer to current models not re-measured`).
- **Methodology**: Statistical and experimental-design first principles (not a claim about model behavior).
- **Project convention**: An engineering convention adopted for this skill, explicitly distinguished from empirical evidence.
- **Contested**: An earlier learned-behavior claim that failed to reproduce or remains statistically inconclusive on `cx/gpt-6-luna`.
- **Unverified / Unsupported**: Quantitative claims or mechanisms lacking retrieved primary-source support are removed from rules and cataloged in [unverified_hypotheses.md](unverified_hypotheses.md).
