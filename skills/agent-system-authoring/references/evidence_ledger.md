# Master Evidence Ledger: System Instructions, Tool Schemas & Skill Packaging

## Overview
This ledger indexes all 28 authoring rule IDs (`R-SYS-01`–`R-SYS-10`, `R-TOOL-01`–`R-TOOL-12`, `R-SKILL-01`–`R-SKILL-06`) alongside their verification tier, newest models tested in primary sources, empirical observations across **Phases A–E** (`3,098` runs on `GPT 6 Luna`), **`H-08`** (`950` runs on `Particle-1` / DeepSeek V4.1 Flash lineage), and **Phase F** (`1,190` cross-architecture runs on `Nemotron 3.5 Lightning` / `30B-A3B` Mamba-2 SSM Hybrid; **`5,238` total benchmark records**, `0` errors in Phases B–F & `H-08`), and primary citations verified in [source_checks.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/source_checks.md). All Phase B–F & `H-08` inferential tests are computed strictly at the **prompt level** (paired *t*-tests `df = P - 1` and exact two-sided sign tests; zero pooled-repeat Fisher p-values).

## Evidence Tier Policy
- **T1 Verified fact**: A specification constraint or API mechanic confirmed directly against source text retrieved on 2026-11-22 / 2026-11-26.
- **T2 Measured empirically**: A statistically significant prompt-level effect recomputed directly from raw execution traces in `evidence/` via `evidence/recompute_stats.py` *(scope explicitly stated per rule: **3 model families & 2 architectures** `GPT 6 Luna` + `Particle-1` + `Nemotron 3.5 Lightning` for `R-SYS-05`, `R-TOOL-04`, `R-TOOL-05`; **2 architectures** `GPT 6 Luna` + `Nemotron 3.5 Lightning` for `R-TOOL-11`; `GPT 6 Luna` single-model for `R-SYS-01`)*.
- **T3 Current vendor guidance**: A recommendation quoted from official vendor documentation retrieved on 2026-11-22 (*not measured by us*).
- **T4 Literature — mechanism**: A peer-reviewed finding grounded in architectural mechanics that have not changed (left-to-right/constrained decoding, BPE tokenization, context-token cost, FP16/BF16 non-determinism).
- **T5 Literature — learned behavior**: A peer-reviewed finding whose effect depends on model training (instruction following, personas, few-shot sensitivity, positional bias, tool-description influence). Kept as a rule and tagged with tested models (`transfer to current models not re-measured`).
- **Methodology**: Statistical and experimental-design first principles (not a claim about model behavior).
- **Project convention**: An engineering convention adopted for this skill, explicitly distinguished from empirical evidence.
- **Contested**: An earlier learned-behavior claim that failed to reproduce or remains statistically inconclusive on `GPT 6 Luna`.
- **Unverified / Unsupported**: Quantitative claims or mechanisms lacking retrieved primary-source support are removed from rules and cataloged in [unverified_hypotheses.md](unverified_hypotheses.md).
