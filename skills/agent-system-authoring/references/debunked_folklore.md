# Debunked Prompting & Tool Schema Folklore

## Executive Summary
This catalog separates prompting and schema conventions into:
1. **Refuted Conventions (`T1`–`T5` / `Methodology`)** contradicted by retrieved literature, vendor specs, statistical methodology, or prompt-level Phase B (`PB-1`–`PB-6`) and Phase C (`PC-1`–`PC-2`) experiments; and
2. **Conditionally Bounded / Inconclusive Comparisons** where experiments refined scope limits or hit ceiling/power limits.

---

## 1. Refuted Conventions

| Folklore / Industry Convention | Conventional Belief | Verified Reality | Supporting Evidence & Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Assign an expert persona ('You are a Fields Medalist...') to boost factual or math accuracy"** | Authoritative role framing improves objective problem-solving accuracy. | **Does not improve overall accuracy over a neutral no-persona prompt.** Zheng et al. found no overall gain across 162 roles, 4 LLM families, and 2,410 questions. In non-ceiling `PB-5` (`low` effort, `n = 6` prompts), `neutral_objective` scored `0.967` (`29/30`, `TARa@5 = 83%`) vs `expert_persona` `0.900` (`27/30`, `TARa@5 = 50%`; prompt-level `t(5) = -1.00, p = 0.3632`, underpowered null consistent with **T5** literature). | **T5** (`R-SYS-06`): Basil et al. ([arXiv:2512.05858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05858)); Zheng et al. ([arXiv:2311.10054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10054)); `PB-5`. |
| **"Always write 'Think step-by-step' on reasoning models"** | Explicit CoT phrases improve all complex prompts universally. | **Unnecessary and can hinder performance on native reasoning models.** Reasoning models deliberate internally before answering; Li et al. observed consistent instruction-following drops under CoT prompting across 15 models on IFEval/ComplexBench. | **T3** (`R-SYS-07`): OpenAI [Reasoning Best Practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices); Li et al. ([arXiv:2505.11423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11423)). |
| **"Long-context LLMs handle arbitrarily large tool catalogs without pruning"** | Large context windows eliminate selection degradation from distractor tools. | **Selection accuracy drops sharply as tool count and response size grow.** LongFuncEval measured `7%` to `85%` performance drops as tool catalogs scaled and `7%` to `91%` drops as tool responses lengthened; Anthropic advises consolidating tools. | **T4** (`R-TOOL-08`): Kate et al. ([arXiv:2505.10570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10570)); Qin et al. ([arXiv:2307.16789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16789)); Anthropic `define-tools`. |
| **"Setting `temperature=0` guarantees deterministic single-run results"** | Greedy decoding produces reproducible outputs without repeats. | **Non-determinism persists due to FP16/BF16 non-associativity and dynamic batching.** Atil et al. measured up to `15%` accuracy variation and `70%` best-worst gaps across 10 runs. `GPT 6 Luna` repeats (`N = 5`) confirmed run-to-run drift: `TARa@5` fell to `17%` (`PB-1 flat_markdown`, `PB-4 affirmative_only`) and `44%` (`PC-2 affirmative_only`). | **Methodology / T4** (`R-SYS-10`): Yuan et al. ([arXiv:2506.09501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09501)); Atil et al. ([arXiv:2408.04667](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04667)); `PB`/`PC`. |
| **"Never state negative prohibitions—replace them with affirmative-only suggestions"** | Telling a model what to use instead lets you omit explicit `Do NOT` exclusions cleanly. | **Underspecified affirmative suggestions (`"such as..."`) failed frequently (`65.0%`), while explicit `Do NOT` prohibitions succeeded (`98.8%`).** In `PC-2` (`16` prompts × `5` = `80`/arm), `bare_negative` (`0.988`, `79/80`) and `paired_affirmative` (`0.950`, `76/80`) decisively beat unstated-blacklist `affirmative_only` (`0.650`, `52/80`: `t(15)=4.06, p=0.0010` and `t(15)=3.87, p=0.0015`). Substitutes gave no extra lift (`p = 0.3332`). Negative-arm misses (`5/160`) were hyphenated compounds (`consecutive-failure`), so state compound handling explicitly. | **T2** (`R-SYS-05`): `PC-2` & `PB-4` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)). |
| **"More few-shot examples always improve compliance"** | Adding many examples monotonically boosts performance regardless of rule alignment. | **Excessive examples can degrade performance, and conflicting examples override written rules.** Tang et al. demonstrated over-prompting degradation; Camassa & Shiller measured `27%`/`43%` grand-mean rule following across 13 models when assistant turns contradicted rules; OpenAI advises trying zero-shot first on reasoning models. | **T5** (`R-SYS-09`): Tang et al. ([arXiv:2509.13196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13196)); Camassa & Shiller ([arXiv:2605.20382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20382)); OpenAI Reasoning Guide. |
| **"LLMs neutrally ignore promotional fluff when choosing between equivalent tools"** | Tool selection depends purely on functional capability matching. | **Promotional superlatives overrode neutral parallel/position tie-breaks in counterbalanced `PC-1`.** Across `16` counterbalanced prompts (`80`/arm; `east`/`west` naming induced `67/80` parallel dual calls at baseline), assertive text (`"Primary and preferred..."`) flipped exclusive single selection of `second_tool` on **`16/16` prompts (`80/80` runs vs `0/80` neutral/example; exact prompt sign `p = 3.052e-05`)**, aligning with >`10x` shifts across 17 LLMs in Faghih et al. | **T2 / T5** (`R-TOOL-04`): `PC-1` & `PB-2` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Faghih et al. ([arXiv:2505.18135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18135)). |
| **"Constrained JSON decoding has zero reasoning cost when pre-emission thinking is absent"** | Parameter field order in strict schemas never affects computed arguments. | **Forcing constrained action fields before explanation tokens severely degrades accuracy when pre-emission thinking is absent.** In `PB-6` (`effort="none"`, `reasoning_tokens = 0` proxy, `n = 6` prompts), both `no_rationale` and `rationale_last` scored `0.367` (`11/30`), whereas `rationale_first` reached **`1.000` (`30/30`, prompt-level `t(5) = 3.12, p = 0.0261`; sign `4+/0-, p = 0.1250`)**. | **T2 / T4** (`R-TOOL-11`): `PB-6` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Tam et al. ([arXiv:2408.02442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02442)); Nguyen et al. ([arXiv:2601.07525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07525)). |
| **"Typed JSON Schema properties make parameter descriptions unnecessary"** | Types (`"type": "string"`) and parameter names convey sufficient format info. | **Non-universal formatting rules fail without explicit descriptions.** In Phase A `TS-2` (`15` matched prompts, `N = 1`), universal ISO timestamps (`15/15`), ISO currencies (`12/15`, sign `p = 0.2500`), and decimal coordinates (`15/15`) were inferred, but omitting descriptions failed **`0/15` vs `15/15`** purely on the unstated no-space `"lat,lon"` syntax (`sign / McNemar p = 6.104e-05`). | **T2** (`R-TOOL-05`): `TS-2` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Lu et al. ([arXiv:2408.04682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04682)). |
| **"XML containers inherently break OpenAI-lineage reasoning compliance"** | Wrapping rules in XML tags drops accuracy by 15% compared to Markdown headers. | **Phase A `SP-4` artifact caused by a `[P0\|P1\|P2\|P3]` wording bug.** Once wording was equalized in `PB-1` (`n = 6` prompts × `5`), `flat_markdown` (`0.972`), `flat_xml` (`0.983`, `t(5)=1.00, p=0.3632`), and `nested_xml` (`0.994`, `t(5)=2.00, p=0.1019`) showed no XML penalty on `GPT 6 Luna`. | **Refuted internal artifact** (`R-SYS-08`): `PB-1` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); He et al. ([arXiv:2411.10541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10541)). |

## 2. Resolved, Underpowered & Conditionally Bounded Comparisons

| Hypothesis | Observation Across Phase B & Phase C (`GPT 6 Luna`) | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Paired affirmative substitutes vs bare prohibitions (`C-01`, `PC-2`)** | Resolved in powered `PC-2` (`16` prompts × `5`): `bare_negative` (`0.988`, `79/80`) vs `paired_affirmative` (`0.950`, `76/80`) showed **no compliance advantage** for adding substitutes (`delta = -0.038, t(15) = -1.00, p = 0.3332`; sign `p = 1.0000`). Both strongly beat `affirmative_only` (`0.650`, `p <= 0.0015`). | Resolved: explicit `Do NOT` drives effect |
| **Trailing reminders on high-effort reasoning models (`PB-3`)** | `GPT 6 Luna (xhigh)` hit `30/30` (`100%`) ceiling in both `system_only` and `sandwich_reminder` even under ~1,200 words of distractor logs. Prospective memory (`Mittal, 2026`) remains **T5** for standard/lower-effort models. | Ceiling on `xhigh` |
| **Expert personas on combinatorics (`PB-5`)** | `neutral_objective` (`0.967`, `TARa@5 = 83%`) vs `expert_persona` (`0.900`, `TARa@5 = 50%`), `t(5) = -1.00, p = 0.3632`. Underpowered at `n = 6` prompts to independently prove null; rule relies primarily on **T5** literature. | Underpowered null (n=6) |
| **OpenAI vs Anthropic vs Gemini strict schemas (`R-TOOL-10`)** | Official docs verified on 2026-11-22 confirm `additionalProperties: false` + all-keys `required` is OpenAI-specific; Anthropic `strict: true` permits optional params outside `required`, and Gemini uses an OpenAPI subset without `strict: true`. | Provider-scoped (**T1**) |
