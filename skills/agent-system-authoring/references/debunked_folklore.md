# Debunked Prompting & Tool Schema Folklore

## Executive Summary
This catalog separates prompting and schema conventions into:
1. **Refuted Conventions (`T1`–`T5` / `Methodology`)** contradicted by retrieved literature, vendor specs, statistical methodology, or prompt-level Phases B–E (`GPT 6 Luna`), `H-08` (`Particle-1` / DeepSeek V4.1 Flash), and Phase F (`Nemotron 3.5 Lightning` Mamba-2 SSM) experiments; and
2. **Conditionally Bounded / Inconclusive Comparisons** where experiments refined scope limits or hit ceiling/power limits.

---

## 1. Refuted Conventions

| Folklore / Industry Convention | Conventional Belief | Verified Reality | Supporting Evidence & Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Assign an expert persona ('You are a Fields Medalist...') to boost factual or math accuracy"** | Authoritative role framing improves objective problem-solving accuracy. | **Does not improve overall accuracy over a neutral no-persona prompt.** Zheng et al. found no overall gain across 162 roles, 4 LLM families, and 2,410 questions. In powered non-ceiling `PE-3` (`16` competition discrete-math puzzles × `5`, `high` effort), `neutral_objective` (`72/80`, `90.0%`) vs `expert_persona` (`73/80`, `91.2%`) confirmed a null (`t(15) = 0.22, p = 0.8269`; `sign 2+/1-, p = 1.0000`), matching `PB-5` (`0.967` vs `0.900`, `p = 0.3632`). | **T5** (`R-SYS-06`): Basil et al. ([arXiv:2512.05858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05858)); Zheng et al. ([arXiv:2311.10054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10054)); `PE-3`, `PB-5`. |
| **"Always write 'Think step-by-step' on reasoning models"** | Explicit CoT phrases improve all complex prompts universally. | **Unnecessary and can hinder performance on native reasoning models.** Reasoning models deliberate internally before answering; Li et al. observed consistent instruction-following drops under CoT prompting across 15 models on IFEval/ComplexBench. | **T3** (`R-SYS-07`): OpenAI [Reasoning Best Practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices); Li et al. ([arXiv:2505.11423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11423)). |
| **"Long-context LLMs handle arbitrarily large tool catalogs without pruning"** | Large context windows eliminate selection degradation from distractor tools. | **Selection accuracy drops sharply as tool count and response size grow.** LongFuncEval measured `7%` to `85%` performance drops as tool catalogs scaled and `7%` to `91%` drops as tool responses lengthened; Anthropic advises consolidating tools. | **T4** (`R-TOOL-08`): Kate et al. ([arXiv:2505.10570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10570)); Qin et al. ([arXiv:2307.16789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16789)); Anthropic `define-tools`. |
| **"Setting `temperature=0` guarantees deterministic single-run results"** | Greedy decoding produces reproducible outputs without repeats. | **Non-determinism persists due to FP16/BF16 non-associativity and dynamic batching.** Atil et al. measured up to `15%` accuracy variation and `70%` best-worst gaps across 10 runs. `GPT 6 Luna` and `Particle-1` repeats (`N = 5`) confirmed run-to-run drift: `TARa@5` fell to `0%` (`PE-1`, `PD-4`), `17%` (`PB-1`, `PB-4`), and `44%–50%` (`PC-2`, `H08-PC2`). | **Methodology / T4** (`R-SYS-10`): Yuan et al. ([arXiv:2506.09501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09501)); Atil et al. ([arXiv:2408.04667](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04667)); Phases B–E, `H-08`. |
| **"Never state negative prohibitions—replace them with affirmative-only suggestions"** | Telling a model what to use instead lets you omit explicit `Do NOT` exclusions cleanly. | **Confirmed across THREE model families & 2 architectures (`GPT 6 Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron 3.5 Lightning`): unstated-blacklist affirmative suggestions failed (`53.8%–65.0%`), while explicit `Do NOT` bans succeeded (`88.8%–100.0%`, `p <= 0.0013` on all 3).** Substitutes gave zero lexical-exclusion lift on all 3 (`p >= 0.33`; `+91.1` extra rtok on `Particle-1`), though pairing substitutes helped small non-reasoning prose fluency (`Nemotron` `length_ok` `58.8% -> 70.0%`). | **T2 (3 families)** (`R-SYS-05`): `PC-2`, `H08-PC2`, `NEMO-PC2` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)). |
| **"More few-shot examples always improve compliance"** | Adding many examples monotonically boosts performance regardless of rule alignment. | **Excessive examples can degrade performance, and conflicting examples override written rules.** Tang et al. demonstrated over-prompting degradation; Camassa & Shiller measured `27%`/`43%` grand-mean rule following across 13 models when assistant turns contradicted rules; OpenAI advises trying zero-shot first on reasoning models. | **T5** (`R-SYS-09`): Tang et al. ([arXiv:2509.13196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13196)); Camassa & Shiller ([arXiv:2605.20382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20382)); OpenAI Reasoning Guide. |
| **"LLMs neutrally ignore promotional fluff when choosing between equivalent tools"** | Tool selection depends purely on functional capability matching. | **Confirmed across THREE model families & 2 architectures (`GPT 6 Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron 3.5 Lightning`): promotional superlatives hijacked `second_tool` on `16/16` prompts on all 3 families** (`80/80` Luna, `75/80` `Particle-1` `p=4.79e-15`, `79/80` `Nemotron` `p=4.77e-17`, overriding both parallel hedging and Position-0 primacy while cutting `Nemotron` rtok `-51.1`), matching Faghih et al. across 17 LLMs. | **T2 (3 families) / T5** (`R-TOOL-04`): `PC-1`, `H08-PC1`, `NEMO-PC1` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Faghih et al. ([arXiv:2505.18135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18135)). |
| **"Constrained JSON decoding has zero reasoning cost when pre-emission thinking is absent"** | Parameter field order in strict schemas never affects computed arguments. | **Confirmed across TWO architectures (`Transformer` + `Mamba-2 SSM`) at `rtok = 0`:** in powered `NEMO-PB6` (`16` × `5`), both `no_rationale` and `rationale_last` scored **`0/80` (`0.0%`)** vs **`rationale_first` `69/80` (`86.2%`, `t(15)=19.75, p=3.773e-12`, `sign=16+/0-, p=3.052e-05`)**, matching `PB-6` on Luna (`11/30 -> 30/30`, `p=0.0261`). | **T2 (2 archs) / T4** (`R-TOOL-11`): `NEMO-PB6` & `PB-6` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Tam et al. ([arXiv:2408.02442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02442)); Nguyen et al. ([arXiv:2601.07525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07525)). |
| **"Typed JSON Schema properties make parameter descriptions unnecessary"** | Types (`"type": "string"`) and parameter names convey sufficient format info. | **Confirmed across THREE model families & 2 architectures (`Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron`): omitting descriptions collapsed validity to `2/75` (`2.7%`), `5/75` (`6.7%`), and `6/75` (`8.0%`, `p=8.09e-13`; ALL 3 fields degraded on `Nemotron`) vs `75/75` (`100%`) with descriptions**, while cutting reasoning tokens on all 3 (`-5.6`, `-19.7`, **`-469.5` [`-45%`]**). | **T2 (3 families)** (`R-TOOL-05`): `PD-1`, `H08-PD1`, `NEMO-PD1` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Lu et al. ([arXiv:2408.04682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04682)). |
| **"XML containers inherently break OpenAI-lineage reasoning compliance"** | Wrapping rules in XML tags drops accuracy by 15% compared to Markdown headers. | **Phase A `SP-4` artifact caused by a `[P0\|P1\|P2\|P3]` wording bug.** Once wording was equalized in `PB-1` (`n = 6` prompts × `5`), `flat_markdown` (`0.972`), `flat_xml` (`0.983`, `t(5)=1.00, p=0.3632`), and `nested_xml` (`0.994`, `t(5)=2.00, p=0.1019`) showed no XML penalty on `GPT 6 Luna`. | **Refuted internal artifact** (`R-SYS-08`): `PB-1` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); He et al. ([arXiv:2411.10541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10541)). |
| **"Arabic digits always outperform spelled-out numbers for exact count constraints"** | Digit numerals (`5`, `8`) yield higher exact-count compliance than spelled words (`five`, `eight`). | **No advantage across THREE model families (`Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron`).** Non-ceiling zero-reasoning tests on both `Luna` (`0.792` vs `0.801`, `p=0.3431`) and `Nemotron` (`0.483` vs `0.509`, `p=0.2535`) plus `Particle-1` ceiling (`80/80`) showed zero digit advantage. | **T2 Null (3 families)** (`H-06`): `PD-4`, `H08-PD4`, `NEMO-PD4` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)). |

## 2. Resolved, Underpowered & Conditionally Bounded Comparisons

| Hypothesis | Observation Across Phases B–E (`GPT 6 Luna`) & `H-08` (`Particle-1`) | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Simultaneous behavioral rule density (`PE-1` / `R-SYS-01`)** | Scaling `10 -> 20 -> 50` mutually satisfiable rules on `GPT 6 Luna (high)` dropped `strict_all` **`100.0%` (`80/80`) -> `48.8%` (`p=5.38e-08`) -> `58.8%` (`t(15)=-7.34, p=2.44e-06`, `sign p=3.05e-05`)** and multiplied `rtok` **`3.5×` (`p=2.75e-12`)** (`core5=100%` primacy; `rules_05` drifted on `R02` word count before `R06–08` entered). | Confirmed **T2** on Luna |
| **Paired affirmative substitutes vs bare prohibitions (`C-01`, `PC-2`, `H08-PC2`)** | Resolved across **2 families** (`16` × `5`): `paired` vs `bare` gave zero accuracy edge on Luna (`p = 0.3332`) and tied at `80/80` on `Particle-1` while burning `+91.1` extra `rtok` (`p = 6.58e-05`). | Resolved (2 families) |
| **Parameter description verbosity ceiling (`H-02b`, `PD-2`)** | Across `16` prompts × `5` (`effort="none"`), `compact_15t` (`412.1` ptok) through `bloated_250t` (`2,375.1`) all hit **`80/80` (`100.0%`)** — benign verbosity incurs **`5.8×` prompt-token bloat** (`+1,963` tok/call) for zero accuracy gain. | Resolved on Luna: pure token tax |
| **Markdown syntax in tool descriptions (`H-03`, `PD-3`)** | With wording equalized (`16` × `5`, `effort="none"`), `plain_prose` (`0.950`), `bullet_list` (`0.950`, `p=1.0000`), and `markdown_headers` (`0.975`, `p=0.3332`) showed no significant routing gain while `###` headers added `+43.4` ptok (`p=1.69e-20`) and `+0.38s` latency (`p=0.0149`). | Resolved null on Luna (prose optimal) |
| **Prompt-injected TypeScript vs JSON Schema (`H-07`, `PD-5`, `H08-PD5`)** | Outside native JSON Schema tool APIs (`T1`), `typescript_interface` matched `json_schema` at **`80/80` (`100.0%`)** on **both** Luna (`-50.4%` ptok, `-516` tok/call) and `Particle-1` (`-49.2%` ptok, `-535` tok/call, `+13.2` rtok, net `-521.8` total tok). | Resolved (2 families, prompt-only) |
| **Trailing reminders on high-effort reasoning models (`C-02`, `PB-3`, `PE-2`)** | Even at `~10.9k` noisy log tokens (`PE-2`, `high`), `system_only` (`0.885`) vs `sandwich_reminder` (`0.870`, `p=0.6062`) held `80/80` (`100%`) pure formatting in both arms (all misses were counting arithmetic). Prospective memory (`Mittal, 2026`) remains **T5** for standard/multi-turn models. | Null on `Luna (high)` |
| **Start/Middle/End needle + injection (`C-04`, `PE-4`)** | At `~2.8k` prompt tokens (`18` docs, `high`), Start/Middle/End all hit `80/80` (`100%`) on recall and prompt-injection resistance. Near-limit (>50% context) placement trade-offs remain **T5**. | Ceiling at `~2.8k` tokens |
| **OpenAI vs Anthropic vs Gemini strict schemas (`R-TOOL-10`)** | Official docs verified on 2026-11-22 & 2026-11-26 confirm `additionalProperties: false` + all-keys `required` is OpenAI-specific; Anthropic `strict: true` permits optional params outside `required`, and Gemini supports standard JSON Schema via `parametersJsonSchema` (or legacy OpenAPI-subset `parameters`) without `strict: true`. | Provider-scoped (**T1**) |
