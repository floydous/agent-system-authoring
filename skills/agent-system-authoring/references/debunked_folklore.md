# Debunked Prompting & Tool Schema Folklore

## Executive Summary
This catalog separates prompting and schema conventions into:
1. **Refuted Conventions (`T1`–`T5` / `Methodology`)** contradicted by retrieved literature, vendor specs, statistical methodology, or prompt-level experiments across **4 model families & 3 architectures** (`GPT 6 Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron 3.5 Lightning` Mamba-2 SSM, `Ling 3.2 Flash` Hybrid-Linear MoE); and
2. **Conditionally Bounded / Inconclusive Comparisons** where experiments refined scope limits or hit ceiling/power limits.

---

## 1. Refuted Conventions

| Folklore / Industry Convention | Conventional Belief | Verified Reality | Supporting Evidence & Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Assign an expert persona ('You are a Fields Medalist...') to boost factual or math accuracy"** | Authoritative role framing improves objective problem-solving accuracy. | **Does not improve overall accuracy over a neutral no-persona prompt.** Zheng et al. found no overall gain across 162 roles, 4 LLM families, and 2,410 questions. In powered non-ceiling `PE-3` (`16` competition discrete-math puzzles × `5`, `high` effort), `neutral_objective` (`72/80`, `90.0%`) vs `expert_persona` (`73/80`, `91.2%`) confirmed a null (`t(15) = 0.22, p = 0.8269`; `sign 2+/1-, p = 1.0000`), matching `PB-5` (`0.967` vs `0.900`, `p = 0.3632`). | **T5** (`R-SYS-06`): Basil et al. ([arXiv:2512.05858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05858)); Zheng et al. ([arXiv:2311.10054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10054)); `PE-3`, `PB-5`. |
| **"Always write 'Think step-by-step' on reasoning models"** | Explicit CoT phrases improve all complex prompts universally. | **Unnecessary and can hinder performance on native reasoning models.** Reasoning models deliberate internally before answering; Li et al. observed drops across 15 models on IFEval/ComplexBench. Powered non-ceiling `LUNA-PG2` (`8` math + `8` constrained × `5`) confirmed zero lift (`direct_goal` `0.904` vs hidden `0.931` `p=0.3992` & visible `0.894` `p=0.8109`). | **T3** (`R-SYS-07`): OpenAI [Reasoning Best Practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices); Li et al. ([arXiv:2505.11423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11423)); `LUNA-PG2`. |
| **"Long-context LLMs handle arbitrarily large tool catalogs without pruning"** | Large context windows eliminate selection degradation and cost from distractor tools. | **Unpruned catalogs impose steep token bloat (`12.8×` in `LUNA-PG1`) and degrade selection on many models.** `GPT 6 Luna` held `100%` (`80/80`) up to `100` nested tools in `LUNA-PG1` (`effort="none"`) but burned **`12.8×` prompt tokens (`374.8 -> 4,797.9`, `p=1.83e-33`)**; LongFuncEval measured `7%`–`85%` accuracy drops as catalogs scaled. | **T4** (`R-TOOL-08`): `LUNA-PG1` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Kate et al. ([arXiv:2505.10570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10570)); Qin et al. ([arXiv:2307.16789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16789)); Anthropic `define-tools`. |
| **"Setting `temperature=0` guarantees deterministic single-run results"** | Greedy decoding produces reproducible outputs without repeats. | **Non-determinism persists due to FP16/BF16 non-associativity and dynamic batching.** Atil et al. measured up to `15%` accuracy variation and `70%` best-worst gaps across 10 runs. `GPT 6 Luna` and `Particle-1` repeats (`N = 5`) confirmed run-to-run drift: `TARa@5` fell to `0%` (`PE-1`, `PD-4`), `17%` (`PB-1`, `PB-4`), and `44%–50%` (`PC-2`, `H08-PC2`). | **Methodology / T4** (`R-SYS-10`): Yuan et al. ([arXiv:2506.09501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09501)); Atil et al. ([arXiv:2408.04667](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04667)); Phases B–G, `H-08`. |
| **"Never state negative prohibitions—replace them with affirmative-only suggestions"** | Telling a model what to use instead lets you omit explicit `Do NOT` exclusions cleanly. | **Confirmed across FOUR model families & 3 architectures (`Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron`, `Ling`): unstated-blacklist affirmative suggestions failed (`53.8%–72.5%`), while explicit `Do NOT` bans succeeded (`88.8%–100.0%`, `p <= 0.0013` on all 4).** Substitutes gave zero lexical-exclusion lift on all 4 (`p >= 0.33`). | **T2 (4 families)** (`R-SYS-05`): `PC-2`, `H08-PC2`, `NEMO-PC2`, `LING-PC2` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)). |
| **"More few-shot examples always improve compliance"** | Adding many examples monotonically boosts performance regardless of rule alignment. | **Excessive examples burn tokens or degrade accuracy, and conflicting examples override weaker models or inflate thinking.** Camassa & Shiller measured `27%`/`43%` compliance across 13 models on conflicting shots. In `LUNA-PG3`, `zero_shot` hit `80/80` (`-255` net tok vs `aligned_3shot`, `p=0.3332`), and `conflicting_3shot` failed to hijack Luna (`0/80`) but burned **`+86%` extra `rtok` vs aligned (`p=3.36e-11`)**. | **T5** (`R-SYS-09`): Tang et al. ([arXiv:2509.13196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13196)); Camassa & Shiller ([arXiv:2605.20382](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20382)); OpenAI Guide; `LUNA-PG3`. |
| **"LLMs neutrally ignore promotional fluff when choosing between equivalent tools"** | Tool selection depends purely on functional capability matching. | **Confirmed across FOUR model families & 3 architectures (`Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron`, `Ling`): promotional superlatives hijacked `second_tool` on `16/16` prompts on every family** (`80/80` Luna, `75/80` `Particle-1`, `79/80` `Nemotron`, `80/80` `Ling`, cutting `rtok` on `Nemotron` `-51.1` & `Ling` `-47.7`), matching Faghih et al. across 17 LLMs. | **T2 (4 families) / T5** (`R-TOOL-04`): `PC-1`, `H08-PC1`, `NEMO-PC1`, `LING-PC1` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Faghih et al. ([arXiv:2505.18135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18135)). |
| **"Constrained JSON decoding has zero reasoning cost when pre-emission thinking is absent"** | Parameter field order in strict schemas never affects computed arguments. | **Confirmed across THREE architectures (`Transformer` + `Mamba-2 SSM` + `KDA+MLA MoE`) at `rtok = 0`:** `rationale_first` jumped from `no_rationale` / `rationale_last` on `Luna` (`36.7% -> 100%`), `Nemotron` (`0% -> 86.2%`, `p=3.77e-12`), and `Ling` (`30.0%/36.2% -> 95.0%`, `p=0.0002`). | **T2 (3 archs) / T4** (`R-TOOL-11`): `LING-PB6`, `NEMO-PB6`, `PB-6` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Tam et al. ([arXiv:2408.02442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02442)); Nguyen et al. ([arXiv:2601.07525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07525)). |
| **"Typed JSON Schema properties make parameter descriptions unnecessary"** | Types (`"type": "string"`) and parameter names convey sufficient format info. | **Confirmed across FOUR model families & 3 architectures (`Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron`, `Ling`): omitting descriptions collapsed validity to `2.7%`, `6.7%`, `8.0%`, and `5.3%` vs `100%` (`75/75`) with descriptions**, while cutting reasoning tokens on all 4 (`-5.6`, `-19.7`, `-469.5`, `-20.8`). | **T2 (4 families)** (`R-TOOL-05`): `PD-1`, `H08-PD1`, `NEMO-PD1`, `LING-PD1` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); Lu et al. ([arXiv:2408.04682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04682)). |
| **"XML containers inherently break OpenAI-lineage reasoning compliance"** | Wrapping rules in XML tags drops accuracy by 15% compared to Markdown headers. | **Phase A `SP-4` artifact caused by a `[P0\|P1\|P2\|P3]` wording bug.** Once wording was equalized in `PB-1` (`n = 6` prompts × `5`), `flat_markdown` (`0.972`), `flat_xml` (`0.983`, `t(5)=1.00, p=0.3632`), and `nested_xml` (`0.994`, `t(5)=2.00, p=0.1019`) showed no XML penalty on `GPT 6 Luna`. | **Refuted internal artifact** (`R-SYS-08`): `PB-1` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)); He et al. ([arXiv:2411.10541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10541)). |
| **"Arabic digits always outperform spelled-out numbers for exact count constraints"** | Digit numerals (`5`, `8`) yield higher exact-count compliance than spelled words (`five`, `eight`). | **Refuted across FOUR model families (`Luna`, `Particle-1`, `Nemotron`, `Ling`).** Non-ceiling zero-reasoning tests showed null composite differences on `Luna` (`p=0.3431`), `Nemotron` (`p=0.2535`), and `Ling` (`p=0.3489`; `strict_all` actually favored `spelled_words` `32.5%` vs `16.2%`, `p=0.0179`). | **T2 Refuted (4 families)** (`H-06`): `PD-4`, `H08-PD4`, `NEMO-PD4`, `LING-PD4` ([stats_recomputed.md](https://github.com/floydous/agent-system-authoring/blob/main/evidence/stats_recomputed.md)). |

## 2. Resolved, Underpowered & Conditionally Bounded Comparisons

| Hypothesis | Observation Across Phases B–G & `H-08` (4 Model Families, 3 Architectures) | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Simultaneous behavioral rule density (`PE-1`, `LING-PE1` / `R-SYS-01`)** | Confirmed across **2 architectures**: Luna (`high`) `10 -> 50` cut `strict_all` `100% -> 58.8%` (`p=2.44e-06`) & `3.5×` `rtok`; `Ling` (`thinking_off`) collapsed `strict_all` monotonically **`97.5% -> 55.0% -> 32.5% -> 16.2%` (`p=1.48e-10`)** and eroded `core5` `0.995 -> 0.840`. | Confirmed **T2 (2 archs)** |
| **Paired affirmative substitutes vs bare prohibitions (`C-01`, `PC-2`, `H08`/`NEMO`/`LING`)** | Resolved across **4 families**: `paired` vs `bare` gave zero lexical-exclusion edge on all 4 (`p >= 0.33`; `+91.1` extra `rtok` on `Particle-1`), though substitutes aided small non-reasoning prose length on `Nemotron`. | Resolved (4 families) |
| **Parameter description verbosity ceiling (`H-02b`, `PD-2`)** | Across `16` prompts × `5` (`effort="none"`), `compact_15t` (`412.1` ptok) through `bloated_250t` (`2,375.1`) all hit **`80/80` (`100.0%`)** — benign verbosity incurs **`5.8×` prompt-token bloat** (`+1,963` tok/call) for zero accuracy gain. | Resolved on Luna: pure token tax |
| **Markdown syntax in tool descriptions (`H-03`, `PD-3`)** | With wording equalized (`16` × `5`, `effort="none"`), `plain_prose` (`0.950`), `bullet_list` (`0.950`, `p=1.0000`), and `markdown_headers` (`0.975`, `p=0.3332`) showed no significant routing gain while `###` headers added `+43.4` ptok (`p=1.69e-20`) and `+0.38s` latency (`p=0.0149`). | Resolved null on Luna (prose optimal) |
| **Prompt-injected TypeScript vs JSON Schema (`H-07`, `PD-5`, `H08-PD5`, `LING-PD5`)** | Outside native JSON Schema tool APIs (`T1`), `typescript_interface` matched `json_schema` at **`80/80` (`100.0%`)** across **3 families**: Luna (`-50.4%`, `-516` ptok), `Particle-1` (`-49.2%`, `-535` ptok), and `Ling` (**`-56.6%`, `-662` ptok**, `p=3.05e-05`). | Resolved (3 families, prompt-only) |
| **Trailing reminders on high-effort reasoning models (`C-02`, `PE-2`, `LUNA-PG4`)** | Both single-turn `PE-2` (`~10.9k` ptok) and `16`-turn `LUNA-PG4` (`~7.6k` ptok, subtle trigger) held `80/80` (`100%`) format accuracy on `Luna (high)`, yet `LUNA-PG4` trailing reminder cut **`rtok` `-27.7%` unanimously (`p=5.16e-09`)** and latency `-0.46s` (`p=0.029`). Accuracy protection remains **T5**. | Token savings on Luna; **T5** accuracy |
| **Start/Middle/End needle + injection (`C-04`, `PE-4`, `LUNA-PG5`)** | Both `PE-4` (`~2.8k` ptok) and `LUNA-PG5` (`~67.3k` ptok / `>50%` window) hit `80/80` (`100%`, `560/560` injections resisted) across all placements; `doc_middle_refreshed` slightly trimmed `rtok` (`-1.6`, `p=0.0222`). Near-limit accuracy decay remains **T5**. | Ceiling at `~67.3k` tokens |
| **OpenAI vs Anthropic vs Gemini strict schemas (`R-TOOL-10`)** | Official docs verified on 2026-11-22 & 2026-11-26 confirm `additionalProperties: false` + all-keys `required` is OpenAI-specific; Anthropic `strict: true` permits optional params outside `required`, and Gemini supports standard JSON Schema via `parametersJsonSchema` (or legacy OpenAPI-subset `parameters`) without `strict: true`. | Provider-scoped (**T1**) |
